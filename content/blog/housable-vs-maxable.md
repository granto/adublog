This URL originally compared Housable and Maxable in October 2020. We have removed the old service chart rather than imply that prices, scope, coverage, or deliverables still match it. This page does not rank the companies or describe their current offerings.

## Compare written scopes today

Ask each provider for the current proposal, service area, deliverables, exclusions, fees, cancellation terms, and responsibility for permits and revisions. Clarify whether a proposal covers feasibility, design, engineering, permit submission, or construction. A plan purchase is not the same thing as a completed unit.

## Use the same checklist for both proposals

List each deliverable and identify who supplies it. Ask what happens if the site needs more engineering, the jurisdiction requests revisions, or utility work exceeds the allowance. Confirm any professional licensing and insurance relevant to the work with the appropriate authority.

Read [how to compare construction-cost ranges](/blog/adu-construction-cost-estimate-range/) for a method that does not depend on an old vendor chart. Seek current written information directly from each company before making a selection.
