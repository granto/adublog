In February 2022, we shared our contribution to Redfin's article about questions to consider before building an ADU. The original link is retained here as an external resource, not as a verified current rulebook or a claim that ADUroi authored all fourteen tips.

## Read the original contribution

[Redfin: 14 expert tips to consider before you build an ADU](https://www.redfin.com/blog/build-an-adu/).

Requirements vary by jurisdiction and project. Check present planning and building requirements with the authority responsible for your property rather than relying on an older article.

## Bring better questions to the first conversation

Ask which site conditions are known, which require an inspection, and what the contractor's scope excludes. Establish whether design, permits, utility work, and contingency appear in the budget. Ask a lender to confirm financing eligibility rather than assuming future rent will qualify.

The financial question is what the ADU changes compared with keeping the property as it is.
